About

I'm a technical writer and content producer for developer tools. I started as a Linux admin, but documentation work was always calling my name. I wrote runbooks, guides, and technical training materials that helped other people understand how the systems worked. Eventually, I made it official and became a technical writer.

After five years writing customer-facing docs for a cloud platform, I moved into developer relations, where I specialized in video tutorials, blog posts, product education, and tech conferences. Early on in my career, I found it hard to decide between pursuing work that was technical and work that was creative. Turns out, technical content creation allows me to do both.

I also do voiceover work and travel advising on the side, and I run Human Readable Media, producing technical video for developer tools companies independently. And as my personal passion project, I run Wander/Ling>, a YouTube channel where I share stories about how language shapes the places I've traveled to.

To sum things up, I love to tell stories. Whether those stories are about technology or world travels, I can't help but share everything I learn.
