About

I found my way to technical writing from systems administration. I had become the team notetaker and runbook writer, and I realized I enjoyed explaining the work more than waking up at 3am to restart servers.

That led to five years writing customer-facing docs for a cloud platform, then a role in developer relations where I was asked to make YouTube tutorials for product use cases. I loved it immediately and kept making more. Somewhere along the way, what started as a hobby became a second career in voice acting. Both are ongoing, but here on this site, you'll find my writing.
